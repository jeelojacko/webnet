## 1. The honest model

Each member is represented by its terminal at the shared joint V (a line, or a
circular arc with signed travel sweep). Its offset at a signed distance `d`
along the grading side is a true geometric offset: a parallel line, or a
concentric arc of radius `R + d·(N·r̂)` (signed — it may reach zero or go
negative). The join is wherever the **two offset curves** intersect. The offset
is never forced through V, and no branch is ever auto-picked: every
intersection is surfaced with its along-travel parameters `uIn`/`uOut`
(relative to V, along each member's own travel direction), `|J−V|`, locality,
and branch consistency.

Branch pattern for a simple corner (derived, then verified against all
fixtures): a GAP join has `uIn ≥ 0` and `uOut ≤ 0`; an OVERLAP join has the
opposite signs. A local intersection that matches is a candidate join; local
co-located multiplicity and degeneracies are classified, never resolved.

Span rule (structural, not parametric): an outside-corner (GAP) join needs
extension past the member ends by construction (`uIn ≥ 0` past the incoming
body, `uOut ≤ 0` before the outgoing body starts), so a GAP join with a
single consistent intersection is `NONLOCAL` (`join-outside-member-span`)
under strict span accounting — it is geometrically located but not usable
on the bodies. That is distinct from an AMBIGUOUS GAP join (`LA_CW_GAP`,
`AL_CW_GAP`, the arc→arc GAP references): ≥2 local branch intersections
survive, so no single join is located and no branch is picked. Only
inside-corner (OVERLAP) joins can land on the bodies as UNIQUE. A short-body variant
(`LL_SPAN_MISMATCH`, 1 m bodies, geometric join at 7.07 m) pins the same gate
from the body-length side.

Coverage: line→line, line→arc, arc→line, arc→arc (reference only); GAP and
OVERLAP (convex/concave); left/right; CW/CCW; equal and unequal arc radii;
shallow (1° and 0.001°), constructed-tangent (unresolved) and coincident-circle
degeneracies, a clean miss, and a span mismatch; translated + rotated copies
of every fixture, plus large-coordinate (E/N ~ 1e6, and 1e8 in the
numerical-authority suite) copies. All seven classifier epsilons are derived

## 3. Locality / extent authority

The only production distance authority is `miterExtent(maxSearchDistance)`,
which bounds the *miter seam ray parameter* (`t ≤ maxSearch/(M·Ni)`). It is
**not** a truthful offset-join gate:

| Fixture | offset-join `|J−V|` | `miterExtent` | bound rejects? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `LL_LEFT_TURN_RIGHT` | 7.07 m | 141.42 m | no (20× loose) |
| `LL_HAIRPIN_NONLOCAL` | 572.97 m (NONLOCAL vs 100 m) | 11459.30 m | no |

`miterExtent` never rejects an honest offset join and its magnitude diverges
from the true join distance by more than an order of magnitude on a near-180°
corner. Locality in this study is bounded exactly by `|J − V| ≤ maxSearchDistance`
(one fixture → UNIQUE at 100 m, NONLOCAL at 1 m). Near-parallel tangents are
recorded as a raw determinant/discriminant (`sin 1° ≈ 0.01745`; exactly tangent
`= 0`); the determinant→0 regime coincides with large join distance or
tangency, so the conditioning metric is the early warning, not a gate.

**POLICY_REQUIRED**

1. **Extent.** Production needs a new, explicitly chosen offset-join extent
   constant (or an explicit declaration that the miter bound is authoritative
   and the offset join is only diagnostic). `miterExtent` does not provide it.
2. **Ambiguity.** 12 fixtures are `AMBIGUOUS`: every arc→arc reference and
half the line→arc cases have ≥2 local branch intersections, plus the two
unresolvable degeneracies (near-tangent contact inside conditioning noise,
coincident offset circles). The study never picks one; a productisation needs
a signed disambiguation rule (e.g. the branch continuous with the chain's own
offset path) before any join could be used.

## 4. Scope boundaries

- Arc×arc fixtures are **geometric offset-join reference only**. Production
  arc×arc remains `NO_GO_TERMINAL_CHORD_ARC_PAIR`; this study does not route,
  build, or certify an arc×arc corner.
- Tolerances: no agreement/conditioning tolerance redefines the physical
  `Roff` sign anywhere — collapse/inversion/nonfinite use exact arithmetic
  (`=== 0`, `< 0`, `!finite`) in both tracks.
- The `WRONG_SIDE` / `SELF_INTERSECTION` labels are reachable only through
  orientation-conflict stress inputs (`incomingSide`/`outgoingSide` overrides,
  legacy/corrupted terminals). In a consistent chain, same-side offsets never
  produce them as an isolated join — they appear as the extra co-local branch,
  which is exactly the `AMBIGUOUS` case.
